# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating user-facing copy, error messages, notifications, or documentation for **LegalEase**, adhere to this brand persona.

## Brand Personality
* **Authoritative yet Approachable:** Inspires legal trust without intimidating clients.
* **Direct & Transparent:** Clear terms, exact fees in integer BDT (৳), and transparent cancellation/refund rules.
* **Empathetic & Discrete:** Recognizes that legal challenges are often stressful; treats all consultations and case materials with strict confidentiality.
* **Jargon-Free:** Explains legal procedures in clear plain language for both English and Bangla speakers.

## Mandatory Legal Disclaimer (Rule BR-20)
Every consultation booking screen, lawyer profile, and email confirmation **MUST** include this exact disclaimer:
> *"This is a preliminary consultation, not formal legal representation."*  
> (Bangla: *"এটি একটি প্রাথমিক পরামর্শ, কোনো আনুষ্ঠানিক আইনি প্রতিনিধিত্ব নয়।"*)

## Grammar & Mechanics Rules
* **Headings:** Title Case for main headers (H1, H2); sentence case for subheadings (H3+).
* **Punctuation:** Never use exclamation marks (!) in error messages, status screens, or transaction summaries.
* **Clarity:** Prefer active voice over passive voice. Keep sentences short and unambiguous.
* **Bilingual Support:** Interface strings should use translation keys. For developer explanations to the user, Bangla is encouraged for conceptual clarity, while code, identifiers, commit messages, and PR descriptions remain strictly in English.

## Terminology Guide

| Do Not Use | Use Instead | Context / Reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Doctor | Lawyer / Advocate | Domain terminology |
| Appointment | Consultation | Domain terminology |
| Specialty | Practice Area | Domain terminology |
| Utilize | Use | Simplicity |
| In order to | To | Simplicity |
| Pay later forever | Pay within 30 minutes | PRD cancellation timeout policy |
| Free consultation | Fixed consultation fee (৳) | Bar Council regulation against free promotions |
| Attorney at Law | Advocate / Lawyer | Bangladesh legal jurisdiction standard |
